# Step 4: The Biscuit problem

Two things came up at the last staff meeting.

First, Maria from intake pointed out that pets are renamed all the time. Many come in unnamed, get a "shelter name" from a volunteer, get a different name from the adopter, and sometimes get renamed again mid-stay if the first name doesn't stick. The current name column just overwrites — the history is lost.

Second, last month a family came in looking for Biscuit, a tabby they had lost two years ago. The shelter had renamed her to Tabitha before her adoption, and the current adopters had renamed her again to Pumpkin. The team couldn't search by "Biscuit" and only solved the case by chance. Dana doesn't want to leave the next reunion to luck.

## Acceptance criteria

- Staff can change a pet's name at any time, and the system records who changed it and when.
- Searching by any name a pet has ever had — current or past — returns that pet.
- If the same name has been held by more than one pet at any point in history, the search surfaces all of them.
